# v0.7.3 — Recommendation Output Contract

## Fixed

- Aggregate Shopping Intelligence verdict now returns `BUY` only when a finalist is `STRONG_RECOMMENDATION` or `RECOMMENDED`.
- Provisional-only (`PROMISING_NEEDS_VERIFICATION`) and caution-only results remain `INSUFFICIENT` and cannot use confirmed-buy wording.
- When the highest-scoring finalist is provisional but a lower-scoring finalist is confirmed, the confirmed finalist becomes the primary candidate for headline recommendation, confidence, reasons, and strengths.
- Missing-information output now includes both structured evidence gaps and candidate tradeoffs.

## Unchanged boundaries

- Recommendation ranking and tier calculation are unchanged.
- Exact-price verification, Provider v2, Relay, authentication, and public-price history are unchanged.

## Verification

- TDD reproduced the original mismatch: finalist existed, backend emitted `BUY` despite `PROMISING_NEEDS_VERIFICATION`.
- Contract tests cover provisional-only, strong recommendation, caution-only, and higher-scoring provisional vs lower-scoring confirmed cases.
